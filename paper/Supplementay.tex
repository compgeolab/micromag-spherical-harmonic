\section*{Supplementary Information}

The micromagnetic models are fully detailed below, providing a complete specification of all 20 simulation geometries, material parameters, discretization details, and resulting net magnetic moments. The simulations were performed using a saturation magnetization ($M_s$) of $480\,000\,\text{A/m}$ (typical for magnetite) and discretized using tetrahedral elements with an average mesh resolution of $9\,\text{nm}$. To ensure reliable relaxed states, a protocol of 100 independent minimizations was performed for each configuration, and the lowest-energy state was selected for analysis. All particles were relaxed without the bias of an external applied field.
